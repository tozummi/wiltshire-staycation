House Rules & Safety

As we’ll be sharing the house with a big group (including little ones), these are just a few simple things to help everyone have a safe, comfortable and enjoyable stay.

Everyone enjoys holidays differently ❤️

Some people love being around everyone all day, while others may need quiet time, rest, or a slower pace. Please remember that different routines and energy levels do not mean someone is being unfriendly, uninterested, or not wanting to be part of things. Please avoid name calling, making comments on someone's appearances, or making assumptions about each other and give everyone space to enjoy the staycation in their own way.

Most importantly:

  • Be kind and patient with one another.
  • Respect each other's routines and personal space.
  • Offer help where you can.
  • If you notice something needs doing, please help rather than waiting for someone else.

Small acts of kindness go a long way when sharing a home together.

Please treat the house as if it were your own 💙

  • Leave shared spaces tidy after use so everyone can enjoy them.
  • Please remove muddy shoes before coming inside.
  • Please wash your hands when returning from outside the property, especially before handling food or spending time with babies.
  • If you spill something, please clean it up straight away.
  • Wipe down surfaces after cooking or messy activities.
  • Please avoid leaving dirty dishes or food out.
  • Return furniture, games and items to where you found them.
  • Please let the group know about any accidental damage — these things happen, and it’s better to know early!

🚭 Smoking & Vaping

Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside the property.

If anyone needs to smoke or vape, please do so outside and dispose of anything responsibly.

We love that everyone wants to spend time with the little ones ❤️ To help keep all children safe:

  • Please ask a parent/carer before giving any food, snacks, sweets or drinks to a child.
  • Please do not offer babies food from your plate without checking first.
  • Please be mindful of choking risks, especially with foods such as whole nuts, popcorn, hard sweets, and small pieces of food.
  • If a child asks for food, please check with their parent first 😊
  • Please keep snacks, drinks and small objects out of reach of little hands.
  • Please tidy toys after play to prevent trips and keep small objects away from babies.
  • Be mindful that toys suitable for older children may not be safe around babies.
  • Please return baby equipment (high chairs, feeding items, toys etc.) clean and where you found it.

Please help keep everyone safe by being mindful of the house:

  • Keep doors and gates closed when needed, especially with toddlers around.
  • Be mindful of children near stairs, balconies and open doors.
  • Please do not leave young children unattended on beds, sofas or raised surfaces.
  • Keep chargers, cables and electrical items safely out of reach of children.
  • Do not overload plug sockets.
  • Please unplug hair straighteners/curlers after use.
  • Keep walkways and escape routes clear.
  • Familiarise yourself with emergency exits and first aid supplies.
  • There is a river near the property, so children must be supervised at all times outdoors.
  • Please do not allow children to play near the river without an adult present.
  • Please remain within the designated garden area and respect the surrounding property.
  • Whoever cooks shouldn’t have to wash up alone ❤️
  • Please clean and put away anything you use straight away.
  • Keep kitchen surfaces clear for others.
  • Label food if it belongs to a particular baby/person/family.
  • Please check before sharing food with children.
  • Be mindful of allergies and dietary requirements.

Most rooms have their own bathrooms, but some bathrooms will be shared.

Please help keep them comfortable for everyone:

  • Leave bathrooms clean and tidy after use.
  • Wipe up any water on the floor to prevent slips.
  • Leave sinks and surfaces tidy.
  • Hang towels up after use.
  • Be mindful that others may need to use the space.

Laundry facilities are shared, so please be considerate:

  • Only wash essential loads where possible.
  • Remove clothes promptly once washing is finished.
  • Please do not leave wet clothes sitting in the machine.
  • Leave the laundry area tidy for the next person.
  • Use the correct bins for rubbish and recycling.
  • Avoid leaving rubbish in bedrooms or shared spaces.
  • Empty bins if they become full.
  • Please check packaging before throwing items away.
  • Please be mindful that babies and young children may be sleeping earlier than others.
  • Keep noise at a reasonable level, especially near bedrooms.
  • Use headphones for loud videos or games when appropriate.
  • Be considerate of those who may need an earlier night or a quieter morning.

Before leaving, please check:

  • Doors are locked.
  • Windows are closed where needed.
  • Appliances are switched off.
  • Personal belongings are taken with you.
